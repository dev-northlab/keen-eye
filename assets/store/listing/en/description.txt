Two pictures look the same – but they are not. Keen Eye is a spot the difference puzzle with hand-drawn scenes that come alive: clouds drift, boats rock on the waves, birds fly – and some differences hide in the motion itself.

Many levels also come as detailed 3D pictures. Switch between living drawings and 3D with one tap.

316 LEVELS, 9 WAYS TO PLAY
• Worlds – 150 levels on the farm, in the city, the forest, the sea and space, with 5 to 10 differences
• Time – the same place in the morning and the evening, in summer and winter: find what really changed, not what the season did
• Memory – study the picture for a few seconds, then find the changes from memory
• Travel – famous places in 32 countries, a fun fact about each and a passport stamp for every win
• Detective – every difference is a clue: collect them and name the culprit in seven cases
• Find the odd one out – spot what doesn't belong in ancient Egypt, Rome, the Viking age and other eras
• Tiny boy – 3D scenes from a little boy's world, and nobody tells you how many differences there are
• Cartoons – funny stories that play right before your eyes: some differences show only for a moment
• Two players on one device – duel, take turns or play together

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Normal mode – beat the clock and earn stars for speed and accuracy
• Calm mode – no timer, no penalties, 3 free hints on every level
• Pinch to zoom both pictures at once
• Hidden golden keys open a bonus treasury
• Light and dark theme, relaxing music
• 32 languages

NO ADS. NO INTERNET. NO DATA.
No ads, no chats, no pop-ups – just you and the pictures. The game works fully offline and collects no data: your progress stays on your device.

The first levels of every mode are free. One purchase unlocks the full game forever – no subscription. It works on all your devices with the same Google account.
